My First Steps into Web Development

Posted on November 17, 2025

Today I created my first HTML page from scratch. It may look simple, but it is an important step in my journey as a future fullstack developer. I learned how to use basic tags like <h1>, <p>, and <a>.

Here are the main things I focused on in this first step:

Why I Chose to Learn Fullstack Development

Posted on November 10, 2025

Fullstack development allows me to understand both the frontend and backend sides of an application. This matches my personality: I like seeing the whole picture, from the user interface to the database.

My main reasons for choosing fullstack development are:

  1. I want to build complete applications from end to end.
  2. I enjoy problem solving both in UI and in server-side logic.
  3. It gives me flexibility for future freelance and side projects.

I am currently learning Java for backend and HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for frontend. In the future, I plan to dive deeper into frameworks like Spring Boot and React.

Balancing Coding with Real Life

Posted on November 3, 2025

Learning to code while working in real life is not always easy. However, I try to stay consistent, even if I only have a few hours each day. Small but regular progress is better than doing nothing.

Here are some simple rules I follow to stay productive:

  • Study at least a little every day, even 30–60 minutes.
  • Break big topics into smaller, manageable tasks.
  • Take notes and keep a list of things I want to revisit.

Sometimes I also read articles on Dev.to or watch short tutorials to stay motivated and discover new ideas.
